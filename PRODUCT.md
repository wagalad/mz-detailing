# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Car owners in Charlotte, NC and the greater Charlotte area who want professional detailing. They're searching on their phone, comparing local shops, and deciding quickly based on visual proof of quality and price clarity. They need to trust the work before they book.

## Product Purpose

Single-page marketing website for MZ Detailing — a mobile/local car detailing business. Goal is dual: showcase the quality of work (build trust) and convert visitors into bookings via phone or email. Success means a visitor lands, sees the work, understands pricing, and contacts the business.

## Brand Personality

Premium, confident, precise. The energy of a craftsman who takes their work seriously — not a budget chain, not a flashy hype brand. Understated luxury. Three words: **sharp, confident, earned**.

## Anti-references

- Budget/coupon detailing sites with clip-art cars and Comic Sans energy
- Overly corporate fleet-service sites (stiff, no personality)
- Loud neon streetwear aesthetics — this is craftsmanship, not a DJ drop
- Generic "local service" templates with stock photos of white vans

## Design Principles

1. **Let the work speak first.** The visual hierarchy leads with quality, not copy. Photos and before/afters anchor the page.
2. **Clarity earns the booking.** Pricing is direct and readable — no hunting, no surprises.
3. **Confidence over persuasion.** No cheesy urgency tactics. The brand is assured, not desperate.
4. **Mobile-first, thumb-friendly.** Most visitors are on their phones. CTAs are always reachable.
5. **Earned premium.** Luxury signals come from restraint, not decoration — dark palette, precise spacing, one strong accent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast text on dark backgrounds. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible and have clear focus states.
